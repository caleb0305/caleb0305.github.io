IT Asset Manager

One place to keep track of every company laptop, phone, SIM card and software subscription — who has it, what happened to it, and what needs attention today.

Technical details for IT

The problem

Nobody is sure who has which laptop or phone. Borrowed items are returned late or not at all. Phone bills grow without anyone noticing, and when someone resigns, their equipment is easy to forget. The spreadsheet is out of date the day after it's updated.

What the app does

A dashboard shows what needs attention today. Each item has its own page showing who has it now and everyone who had it before. Staff hand items out, take them back or send them for repair with a few clicks, and the app fills in the signed accountability form for them.

What changes for your team

When someone resigns, one click lists everything they still hold. Overdue loans and expiring contracts show up before they become problems. Monthly phone costs are totalled per department. And it all runs in the Microsoft 365 you already use — no new system to buy.
