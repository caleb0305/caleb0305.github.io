IT Asset Manager

One place to track every laptop, phone, SIM line and software license — who holds it, what happened to it, and what needs attention today. Built on Power Apps and SharePoint in my own Microsoft 365 tenant, based on an asset system I've built and run at work.

The problem

IT teams lose track of who holds which device, loans go overdue, SIM costs pile up per department, license seats run out, and when someone resigns nobody is sure what they still have. Spreadsheets don't keep a history, and nobody notices until an audit.

What I built

A canvas app with a dashboard, inventory, asset records, an employee custody view, mobile & SIM management, software seats and admin settings. Every action follows the item's status rules and writes to a custody history. Accountability and turnover forms print to PDF and save to SharePoint.

What makes it different

One-click offboarding clears an employee across hardware, SIMs and licenses at once. Phones and SIM lines are tracked separately, so a number keeps its plan when the handset changes. Dropdowns are managed by admins, not hard-coded — and it all runs on standard Microsoft 365.
